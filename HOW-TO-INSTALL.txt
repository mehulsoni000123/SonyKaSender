SONY KA SENDER 1.0.0  -  HOW TO INSTALL AND USE
================================================
Flick photos, videos and files from your Android phone to your Windows PC
over your own Wi-Fi. Nothing goes through the internet or any cloud.
By Mehul Soni (@mehuledits.ai)

YOU NEED BOTH PARTS
  Part 1: the PHONE app    -> SonyKaSender.apk
  Part 2: the PC receiver  -> Sony-Ka-Sender-PC-1.0.0.zip
Phone and PC must be on the SAME Wi-Fi.

PART 1 - INSTALL ON THE PHONE (Android 10 or newer)
1. Download SonyKaSender.apk on your phone (from the Releases page), or
   download it on the PC and send it to the phone.
2. Open the file. Android will say it cannot install apps from this source.
   Tap "Settings" and allow installs from your browser or file manager, just
   for this install. Then tap Install.
3. If Play Protect shows a warning: the app is signed with a debug key, not a
   Play Store key. Tap "More details" > "Install anyway".

PART 2 - INSTALL ON THE PC
1. Install Node.js (free, one time): https://nodejs.org  (LTS version).
2. Download Sony-Ka-Sender-PC-1.0.0.zip, right-click > Extract All, and
   keep the folder (for example C:\Apps\Sony Ka Sender).
3. Double-click "Sony Ka Sender.vbs". A "desk" window opens and the receiver
   starts quietly in the background.
4. If Windows Firewall asks, allow access on private networks (the receiver
   uses port 7777).
   Use "Sony Ka Sender (background).vbs" to start without the window, and
   "Stop Sony Ka Sender.vbs" to stop it.

HOW TO SEND
1. Open Sony Ka Sender on the phone. Your PC should appear in the list.
   If not, type the PC address (for example 192.168.1.20) and add it.
2. Pick photos or files, or use Share > "Flick to PC" from your gallery,
   and flick them toward the PC.
3. They land on the desk window. Files are saved in
   Downloads\Sony Ka Sender on the PC.

TROUBLESHOOTING
- PC not showing on the phone: check both are on the same Wi-Fi, the PC
  receiver is running, and Windows Firewall allows it. Guest or "isolated"
  Wi-Fi networks block this.
- Change the save folder: set the SKS_DIR environment variable.

UNINSTALL
Phone: uninstall like any app. PC: run "Stop Sony Ka Sender.vbs", delete the
folder.
